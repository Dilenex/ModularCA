## Why this exists

ModularCA is released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, and is also offered
under a commercial licence to organisations that cannot accept the AGPL's terms.

Offering software under two licences requires one party to hold sufficient rights in all of it.
The Developer Certificate of Origin certifies that you are entitled to submit your contribution;
it does not grant the rights needed to also offer that contribution commercially. This agreement
supplies those rights.

relicense your own contribution however you wish, for anything, forever. What you grant here is
an additional, parallel licence so the project can continue to be offered on both sets of terms.

## How to sign

Add one line to [CLA-SIGNATURES.md](CLA-SIGNATURES.md) **in your first pull request**, alongside
your code:

```
| @your-handle | Your Full Legal Name | YYYY-MM-DD |
```

Adding that line is how you agree to this document. A CI check refuses any pull request whose
author is not listed, and it reads the file from your own branch — so signing and contributing
happen in the same pull request, with no separate step and nothing to wait for.

Signing once covers all of your future Contributions to the Project.

Signing in the repository rather than through a CLA service is deliberate: the record becomes a
git commit with an author, a timestamp and a signature trail, which is a better audit trail than
a row in a third party's database and does not stop working if that third party does.

Contributions made on behalf of an employer need a separate corporate agreement — raise an issue
and we will sort it out before you spend time on code.
