# CLA signatures

Everyone whose work is in ModularCA, and the date they agreed to
[the Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md).

This file is the provenance record for the project. Because it lives in git, each signature is
tied to a commit with an author, a timestamp and a signature trail — which is a better audit
trail than a third-party service, and one that survives that service being discontinued.

There are no exemptions, including for the maintainer. A record that lists "everyone except the
people we didn't bother recording" cannot answer the only question it exists to answer: *do we
hold the rights to all of this?*

## How to add yourself

Append a line to the table below **in your first pull request**, alongside your code. One
signature covers all your future contributions.

```
| @your-handle | Your Full Legal Name | YYYY-MM-DD |
```

By adding your line you confirm you have read [CLA.md](CLA.md) and agree to it.

Contributing on behalf of an employer needs a separate corporate agreement — open an issue
before you write code, so nobody wastes an afternoon.
